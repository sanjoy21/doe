CPR/AED

American Heart Association Heartsaver AED

Course Length: 6 hours

In the event of a cardiac emergency, this course instructs lay rescuers when to phone 911, how to perform CPR, and use an Automated External Defibrillator (AED) on adults, children or infants. Skills to help relieve choking are also taught. Heartsaver AED is recommended for administrative staff, teachers, paraprofessionals, cafeteria and custodial staff.

***Please Note: A Code Blue Drill will be performed at your school as part of the training program. Please accommodate the instructor appropriately.
